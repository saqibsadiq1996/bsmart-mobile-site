BSmart Mobile Privacy Policy

BSmart Mobile is used for business task management and attachment handling.

Permissions

The app may request access to:

  • Camera - for capturing attachment photos/videos
  • Storage/Media - for selecting and viewing attachments
  • Internet - for syncing data with BSmart services

Data

Uploaded attachments and task data are stored within authorised BSmart systems.
