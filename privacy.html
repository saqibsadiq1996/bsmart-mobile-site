BSmart Mobile Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

BSmart Mobile is designed for operational workflow management, task tracking, and attachment handling. This page explains how the application handles permissions and business-related data.

Permissions

The application may request access to:

  • Camera — to capture photos and videos for task attachments.
  • Media & Storage — to select, preview, and store attachments locally.
  • Internet Access — to sync data securely with authorised BSmart systems.
